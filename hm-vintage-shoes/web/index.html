Good to know

Questions, answered

Which sizes do you stock?

Men's EU 40 to 45. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one. Each product page has a size guide.

Do you deliver outside Kenya?

No. We deliver within Kenya only, to all 47 counties.

Why is M-Pesa the only payment method?

It is how most customers in Kenya prefer to pay, and every payment is approved with your own PIN. There are no card details to enter and nothing to pay on the doorstep.

What if my M-Pesa payment does not go through?

Nothing is taken from your account unless you approve the prompt with your PIN. If the prompt times out or is cancelled, simply try again. If M-Pesa sent you a confirmation SMS but this site did not confirm, keep the SMS as proof of payment and open your order page again.

Are these shoes new?

They are vintage Italian shoes, so each pair has been worn or stored for years. The condition of every pair is shown on its product page.
